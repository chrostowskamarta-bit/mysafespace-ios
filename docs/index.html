Clickable prototype · Phase 1 iOS patient app

MySafeSpace

A calm, private space between visits, for patients of a Polish outpatient mental health clinic. Everything here runs on mock data in your browser.

  • Open Calendar: tap any day to rate your mood and write or record a note for that day.
  • Book a visit: Calendar → Book a visit, pick a doctor and a free time.
  • Join the online visit from Home (the demo visit starts a few minutes after you open the page).
  • Try the lifebuoy button above the tab bar (Need help now), or save an entry like "I don't want to be here anymore" to see on-device crisis support.
  • Open Meds and request a prescription extension, then wait a few seconds for the code.

This is a web recreation of the SwiftUI app for review. Calls and location are simulated. Your entries stay only in this browser.
